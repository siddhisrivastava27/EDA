Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) | SQL Project

##Project Overview

This project focuses on exploring and analyzing a data warehouse using SQL to understand customer demographics, product categories, sales transactions, and overall business performance.

The project uses exploratory SQL queries to examine the structure of the database, calculate key business metrics, identify patterns across customers and products, and analyze sales distribution across different categories and countries.

## Project Objectives

Database Exploration: Explore database tables and columns using INFORMATION_SCHEMA.

Customer Analysis: Analyze customer demographics, including country, gender, age, and total customer count.

Product Analysis: Examine product categories, subcategories, product names, and product costs.

Sales Analysis: Calculate total orders, sales quantity, average price, and revenue.

Date Exploration: Identify the first and last order dates and calculate the overall order date range.

Business Metrics: Generate an overview of key business metrics, including total sales, total quantity, average price, orders, products, and customers.

Category Analysis: Explore product counts, average product costs, and revenue generated by each category.

Customer Activity: Identify the number of customers who have placed orders.
